Vice Ledger: an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PSN until earned; no online trophies). Each trophy carries its official name, description and grade plus an original guide with two named sources; counted trophies get a saved progress stepper against their verified PSN target (250 stunts, 42 range golds, 20 race wins, 100 WAiNK locations, 50 challenges, $250k/$100k counters and more); a 5-stage roadmap with live progress bars runs from story chapters through stranger strands and counted activities to collectible sweeps, 100% completion and the platinum; 14 trophies are flagged missable with reasons drawn from the official descriptions, gathered in an early-warning list beside a next-up queue. Progress (ticks and counters) saves in the browser with export and import, filters cover kind and grade plus search, and a spoiler-safe mode hides story trophies until earned. Static files, no build step, works with the network off.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
